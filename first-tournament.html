First Tournament Guide

A typical scholastic chess tournament is a long, 6-hour day. Here is how to survive and thrive.

The Timeline

Check-In: Arrive at least 30 minutes before the scheduled first round. Find the registration desk to verify your child is on the roster.

The "Skittles" Room: This is the chaotic parent waiting area and player downtime room. Set up your base camp here with snacks and chargers.

Pairings Posted: Before each round, papers are taped to the walls or posted . Find your child's board number and color (White or Black).

The Game: Parents are usually kicked out of the playing hall once the round starts. Wait in the Skittles room. When the game ends, the players raise their hands for a Tournament Director (TD) or report the result to the desk.

Packing List

  • Chess bag with board, pieces, and clock
  • Healthy snacks and water (avoid messy/sticky foods)
  • A book or iPad for *you* (there is a lot of waiting!)
  • Layers of clothing (tournament halls are notoriously cold)
