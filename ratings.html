Understanding Chess Ratings

A player's rating is a number that represents their relative skill level. The most common rating system in the US is managed by the US Chess Federation (USCF).

The Basics

Ratings fluctuate based on tournament performance. If your child wins, their rating goes up. If they lose, it goes down. The amount it changes depends on the rating of their opponent.
