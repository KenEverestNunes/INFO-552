Finding a Vetted Coach

When your child has outgrown the basics, it's time to find a private instructor who can analyze their games and teach advanced strategy.

What to Look For

A great chess player does not always equal a great chess teacher. Look for someone who has specific experience teaching children. Many coaches hold titles like NM (National Master) or GM (Grandmaster), but an Expert-level player who connects well with kids is often better for a beginner than a Grandmaster.

Online vs. In-Person

Highly flexible and often more affordable. Coaches use platforms like Zoom combined with digital chess boards. This opens up your options to world-class teachers globally.

Best for younger children who struggle to focus on a screen. You can usually find local coaches by asking Tournament Directors at your weekend events or contacting local chess clubs.
