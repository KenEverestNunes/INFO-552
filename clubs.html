Local Clubs & Teams

Finding a consistent, welcoming environment to practice is key to your child's chess journey.

School Teams vs. Independent Clubs

School Teams: Often run by a parent volunteer or teacher. These are great for social interaction and team-based state competitions. Check with your PTA or school administration to see if an active club exists.

Independent Clubs: Usually run by titled players or dedicated organizers. These tend to be more competitive and offer formal lessons alongside practice games.

How to Find Affiliate Clubs

Use the official US Chess Federation Club Search tool online to find registered affiliates in your zip code. When evaluating a club, ask the organizer if they have a dedicated "scholastic" (youth) section, as some clubs are mostly for adults.
