Tournament Day Survival Guide

Click the topics below to expand and learn the basics before your first 6-hour tournament day.

A Swiss system tournament means players are never eliminated. Your child will play every round, matched against players with similar running scores.

If a player intentionally touches one of their own pieces, they must move it if a legal move is possible. Remind your child to sit on their hands if they are prone to fidgeting!

Pairing sheets are taped to the wall before each round. Look for your child's name to find their assigned board number and whether they are playing as White or Black.
