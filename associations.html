Professional Associations

American Library Association (ALA)

The ALA is the oldest and largest library association in the world. It provides leadership for the development, promotion, and improvement of library and information services.

Visit ALA Website

Association for Information Science and Technology (ASIS&T)

ASIS&T bridges the gap between information science practice and research. I plan to join this organization to connect with professionals working at the intersection of information, technology, and society.

Visit ASIS&T Website

World Wide Web Consortium (W3C)

While not a traditional membership organization for individuals in the same way, following the W3C is essential for web designers. They develop the international standards for the Web, ensuring long-term growth and accessibility.

Visit W3C Website

Interaction Design Association (IxDA)

A global network dedicated to the professional practice of Interaction Design. As I grow my web design skills, I hope to volunteer with local IxDA chapters to learn more about creating engaging user experiences.

Visit IxDA Website
