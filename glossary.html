Glossary for Parents

Translating the dense, insular jargon of the competitive chess ecosystem.

Key Terms

Bye
When a player does not have an opponent for a round (usually due to an uneven number of players). They receive a free point (or half-point, depending on if it was requested in advance).

TD (Tournament Director)
The official referee of the tournament. If a dispute happens on the board, players must raise their hand for the TD.

Time Control
The amount of time each player is given to complete the game. E.g., "G/30 d5" means each player gets 30 minutes, with a 5-second delay before their clock starts ticking on each move.

En Passant & Castling
Special pawn and king moves. As a parent, you don't need to know how to execute them, just know they are legal moves so you don't panic if your child's opponent does them!
