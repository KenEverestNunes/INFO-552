Tournament Etiquette

Chess has strict behavioral rules. Protect yourself and your child from stressful penalties by following these guidelines.

For Parents

  • Zero Interference: You are a spectator. You cannot speak to your child, signal them, point out a forgotten clock press, or dispute a rule for them while their game is active.
  • Absolute Silence: If you are allowed in the playing hall, do not talk, whisper, or use flash photography. Keep your phone fully silenced.
  • Disputes: If your child thinks their opponent cheated, they—not you—must pause the clock and raise their hand for a Tournament Director.

For Players (Remind your child)

  • Shake hands before and after the game.
  • Say "I adjust" (or "J'adoube") before fixing a crooked piece on the board, otherwise the Touch-Move rule applies.
  • Win with grace, lose with dignity.
